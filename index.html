Research

My long-term goal is to build practical and robust machine learning systems in the real world. My current work focuses on large language model agents and neuro-symbolic learning, especially generalization, reasoning, data efficiency, and trustworthy constraint satisfaction.

LLM Agents

Open-ended planning, embodied decision making, self-evaluation, and reliable tool use in realistic environments.

Neuro-Symbolic Learning

Abductive learning, reasoning shortcuts, verification learning, and grounded imitation under weak supervision.

Robust Learning

Open-set learning, distribution shift, model reuse, and label-efficient adaptation in open environments.
